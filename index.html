Likely a strong fit
  • You're actively running lead ads (Meta / Google) — fresh enquiry flow means a real pool of dead leads behind it.
  • One customer is worth £1k+ — a considered, high-value service. The £150 per-attended fee has to make sense against what a closed deal earns you.

  • Fewer than ~30 enquiries in your export's last 90 days — the day-5 checkpoint becomes an exit, so start smaller: per-attended only, or not at all.
  • No availability for 6–8 weeks — reactivated leads go cold waiting; better to fix the diary first.
